## Generated workshop photographs

The following images were generated as photorealistic workshop scenes because we could not find enough clearly Ghanaian / West African licensed photos for every section. They are **not photographs of real people or NeedleBase customers**, and the page captions say so.

- `split-workshop-*` — generated fashion-workshop interior (seamstresses at machines and a cutting table)
- `split-measure-*` — generated scene of a seamstress measuring a client
- `split-fabric-*` — generated still life of ankara/kente fabric, tape, shears and a phone
- `split-owner-*` — generated scene of a workshop owner with a phone and laptop

Generated: 10 Nov 2026, for NeedleBase landing v12.
